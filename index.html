Can Knockout Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy covers Can Knockout: World Tour for iPhone and iPad. The game has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email address or contacts, and we don't sell personal data.

Ads

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: an ad between some levels, and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels. Watching an ad for a reward stays optional.

AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and information about the ads you see to show and measure ads. iPhone asks whether apps may track you, and if you say no, the advertising identifier isn't shared. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form asks how ads may use your data. You can change that answer later in the game's Settings under Privacy choices, and the tracking answer in iPhone Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and crash reports

The game sends anonymous usage events through Google Firebase Analytics, for example which level you played, whether you cleared it, and which screens and offers you opened. This helps us tune difficulty and fix confusing moments. Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes, with the device model, iOS version and what the game was doing. Firebase also derives an approximate region from your connection. TestFlight builds report to a separate test project.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game records which items you bought so it can deliver them, and purchase events are counted in our analytics without your name or payment details.

iCloud save

Your progress, coins and settings are saved to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud, so you can continue on another iPhone or iPad. This save stays in your iCloud and doesn't reach us.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores go to Apple's leaderboards and achievements, and other players can see your Game Center nickname next to your scores. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when your lives are full or a daily gift is ready. They're created on the device and aren't sent from our servers. You can turn them off in the game's Settings.

Children

Can Knockout is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child's data reached us, write to us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices and requests

You can decline tracking, change your ad consent in Settings, turn off reminders, and delete the game to stop all data collection. For questions, or to ask about or delete data linked to your device, write to us at the address below.
