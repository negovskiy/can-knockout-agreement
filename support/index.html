Tell us your iPhone or iPad model and what happened. We read every email.

New phone or reinstalled the game

  • Sign in to the same iCloud account.
  • Open the game and tap Restore my tour.
  • Or go to Settings, Restore from iCloud.

Remove ads is gone

  • Go to Settings, Restore purchases.
  • Coins, packs and the Tour Card come back with your iCloud save.

A purchase didn't arrive

  • Write to us with the date and the item.
  • Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reminders

  • Turn them on or off in Settings, Reminders.
  • If iPhone blocks them, tap Open Settings next to Reminders.

Ad choices

  • In the game: Settings, Privacy choices, where your region asks for consent.
  • On iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
